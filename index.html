EmpDPO: audio samples

Word-level emphasis control in multilingual zero-shot TTS via direct preference optimization — supplementary audio for the ICASSP 2027 submission.

  • Ours-Base: base diffusion TTS model without emphasis training (plain input only). Ours-SFT: supervised fine-tuning with emphasis markers. Ours-DPO: SFT + direct preference optimization (the proposed system).
  • Fish-S2 (Fish Audio S2 Pro, [emphasis] tag), Qwen3-TTS (CustomVoice, natural-language instruction, preset voices), WordVoice (explicit pitch/energy/duration tokens; English and Chinese only) are public systems evaluated as released.
  • Each row plays the same sentence and reference voice with and without the emphasis marker on the highlighted word. Sentences were sampled at random from the evaluation set (6 per language); each is rendered with two of the eight reference voices used in the evaluation (voice 1, male; voice 3, female). All clips are loudness-normalised to −24 LUFS.
